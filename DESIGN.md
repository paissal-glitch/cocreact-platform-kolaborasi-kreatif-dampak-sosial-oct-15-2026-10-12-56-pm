# CoCreAct Design Direction

> Design system, brand personality, and aesthetic guidelines for CoCreAct.

## Identity & Philosophy
- **Name**: CoCreAct (Collaboration · Creative · Action)
- **Tagline**: "Bukan Siapa, Tetapi Apa." / "Not Who, But What."
- **Core Stance**: Community-rooted cross-sector collaboration platform connecting individuals, grassroots groups, educators, creative professionals, and institutions into impactful programs.

## Design Dials (antislop Part 3)
- **ENERGY: 2 (Balanced)** — Bold editorial confidence without being frantic. Energetic electric green highlights against grounded deep forest and charcoal surfaces.
- **RHYTHM: 2 (Varied Cadence)** — Sections change composition purposefully (full-bleed documentary hero -> asymmetric editorial narrative -> structured pillar showcase -> interactive project archive -> centered statement -> step-by-step process -> qualitative impact areas -> direct final call to action).
- **MOTION: 1 (Calm & Purposeful)** — Subtle micro-interactions, smooth hover elevations, accessible typing line with full screen-reader accessibility and strict `prefers-reduced-motion` compliance.

## Color Palette
- **Electric Green (`#39FF14`)**: Signature accent representing creative activation and growth. Used intentionally on key CTA focal points, active states, and focus indicators.
- **Deep Charcoal (`#111411`)**: Grounded dark surface providing deep contrast and cinematic framing.
- **Deep Forest Green (`#102B1B`)**: Secondary brand surface for badges, philosophy breakout, and subtle containers.
- **Warm Off-White (`#F5F7F0`)**: Editorial canvas background for readability sections, text contrast ratio > 17:1 against charcoal.
- **Accessible Muted Green-Grey (`#5A6558`)**: Subtitle and meta text on light surfaces ensuring minimum 6.11:1 WCAG AAA contrast ratio.
- **Warm Coral/Orange (`#E9A15A`)**: Milestone accent for process steps and special annotations.

## Typography
- **Display**: Space Grotesk (geometric, contemporary, humanist sans-serif with distinct character).
- **Body**: Manrope (clean, legible, highly readable at all sizes across mobile and desktop).
- **Meta / Accents**: UI Monospace (precise timestamps, categories, and verified documentation badges).

## Voice & Copywriting
- Human, grounded, evidence-driven.
- Zero generic AI buzzwords ("seamless", "cutting-edge", "revolutionary").
- Strict prohibition of em dash (`—`) per antislop R-02; using commas, colons, or bullets instead.
- Real documented collaborative case studies with verifiable partners and photo archives.
